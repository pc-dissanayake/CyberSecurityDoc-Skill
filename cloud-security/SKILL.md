# Cloud Security

Review cloud resources with a focus on least privilege, exposure boundaries, and evidence of effective controls.

## Review checklist

- IAM policies and trust relations
- public exposure of services and data
- storage permissions and encryption
- network controls and firewall policies
- role design and privilege boundaries
- secrets, keys, and credential handling
- logging, audit trails, and alerting
- backup and recovery reliability

## Important principle

Public reachability is not automatically a vulnerability. Evaluate the purpose of the exposure and the compensating controls in place.
