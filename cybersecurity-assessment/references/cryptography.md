# Cryptography

## Review areas

- TLS configuration and certificate validity
- algorithm selection and key length
- weak ciphers or deprecated protocols
- key management and rotation
- storage of secrets and credentials
- password hashing strength
- token signing and encryption choices

## Assessment questions

- Are cryptographic algorithms current and well configured?
- Are keys stored securely and rotated regularly?
- Does the system fall back to insecure defaults or old protocols?
- Are password hashes resistant to offline attacks?

## Reporting note

Do not use the presence of encryption alone as evidence of strong security. The real quality depends on algorithm choice, implementation quality, key management, and lifecycle controls.
