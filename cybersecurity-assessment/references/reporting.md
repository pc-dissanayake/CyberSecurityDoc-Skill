# Reporting and Severity

## Severity model

Use the following classes when appropriate:

- Critical: likely to result in direct compromise of sensitive systems or major data loss with little user interaction
- High: severe impact with realistic exploitation paths or significant business impact
- Medium: notable security concern requiring remediation within a reasonable time window
- Low: limited impact, reduced exploitability, or weak control with minimal consequence
- Informational: observation that improves security posture without clear risk

## Reporting structure

Every finding should include:

- ID and title
- asset and component
- risk statement
- evidence summary
- technical explanation
- business impact
- severity and rationale
- remediation plan
- verification guidance

## Tone

The report should be disciplined, evidence-based, and actionable rather than alarmist. It should document the facts, explain the impact, and support prioritized remediation.
